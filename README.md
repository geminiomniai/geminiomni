## 🌟 What is Gemini Omni?

[Gemini Omni](https://geminiomni.video/) is a cutting-edge multimodal video generation platform built natively on Google DeepMind's architecture. Unlike traditional tools that process inputs in silos, Gemini Omni reasons across **text, images, audio, and video simultaneously**. 

With a single inference pass, it outputs coherent, physics-aware video content with perfectly synchronized audio. Whether you need cinematic footage, iterative natural-language editing, or ultra-realistic spatial dynamics, Gemini Omni is redefining the future of AI video creation.

## ✨ Key Features

* **🧠 True Multimodal Reasoning:** Seamlessly process complex input combinations simultaneously. Combine text prompts, reference images (up to 9), audio clips (up to 3), and video footage (up to 3) for highly context-aware generation.
* **🎵 Native Audio Synchronization:** Skip post-production. Directly generate ambient sound effects, professional voiceovers, and background music that natively sync with your video's action.
* **🌍 Physics-Aware Rendering:** Experience hyper-realistic motion, lighting, and gravity simulations powered by advanced world-building models that understand real-world spatial relationships.
* **💬 Conversational Editing:** Accelerate your iteration process using natural language. Simply type "make the lighting warmer," "replace the background," or "switch to a close-up shot" to instantly refine the scene without regenerating the entire video from scratch.
* **🎬 Cinematic Output Quality:** Export in stunning 1080p by default, with seamless upscaling options to 2K and 4K resolution to meet professional commercial delivery standards.
